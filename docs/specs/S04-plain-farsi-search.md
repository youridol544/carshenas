- Related: ADR-0029, ADR-0043, ADR-0027, S02, S03; `docs/evidence/query-understanding/2026-10-02/` (report, labelling guide, runs), `docs/evidence/query-understanding/2026-10-04/` (the one-step flow)

## Users and goal

A buyer who writes what they want the way they would say it («۲۰۶ تیپ ۲ بدون رنگ زیر ۷۰۰ میلیون», «یه ماشین تمیز و بی‌دردسر می‌خوام») lands on the results with the filters that became already applied and shown, can take any off, and is told which words nobody could read. Nothing is silently dropped and nothing waits for a confirmation: the owner's complaint of 2026-10-04 about the first version (type, press «بفهم», read the understood chips, press again, and in the hero one more step) is the reason this is one step.

## Flow

1. The buyer types a sentence in the one box, on the home page's hero (the «جست‌وجو» tab) or on the search page, and presses Enter or the box's one button. The example sentences under the hero's box submit by themselves (one click shows results). There is no «understood» panel to approve, no disclosure, no second button.
2. The box's form is a Server Action, `askSearchAction` (`features/search-understanding/search-understanding-actions.ts`). It reads the sentence by code in the server's process (the lexicon is kept five minutes), settles the words no filter could name against the live counts (below), and answers with the canonical address of the search with the sentence kept beside it: `/search?model=peugeot.206&price=..700000000&nopaint=1&ask=<the sentence>`. The page's script goes there with the router, one history entry: the page it leaves keeps what was typed, so Back brings it back; on the search page the box keeps focus on a screen with a keyboard and, on a touch screen, the keyboard closes and focus lands on the count of the results. A form sent before the script has loaded (or with none) is posted and the server redirects (303). A pasted link goes to its check (`/check?link=`), as the box does for one in the browser.
3. The page shows the results, and at the top of them the applied filters as removable chips in plain words, the words nobody could read as one quiet chip when listings have them, and quiet lines only when there is something to say: what the code did with a word (a typo read, Tehran is the market, a city or a make that is not covered, a number no car has), the words left out because they would empty the results (each with «برگرداندن»), the readings a model was not sure of (not applied, one tap to add) and, while a model reads on, one line saying so.
4. Removing a chip updates the results at once. The sentence stays in the box and in the address through every chip, filter and order change, and stays editable: Enter reads it again, and a new sentence replaces the whole search (the filters of the old one are not carried over). «پاک کردن فیلترها» clears every chip, the words and the sentence. The clear button of the box empties the box and lets go of the sentence; the chips stay.

The owner's longer vague example («یک ماشین تمیز کم کار و بیدردسر می‌خوام که همه چیش از نظر فنی خوب باشه …») lands on the catalogue «تمیز و بی‌دردسر»: low mileage for age, popular model, paint free, no accident, no replaced parts, sound engine, gearbox and chassis, each a chip. The hero's shorter example («یک ماشین تمیز، کم‌کارکرد و بی‌دردسر») names no technical condition, so it lands on its five filters (low mileage, popular model, paint free, no accident, no replaced parts), not on the catalogue.

## Rules

- The output is validated against `SearchSchema`, the schema the filter sheet and the URL use. A value it refuses is not applied and its words are shown.
- **Words no filter could name** (`unknown`, and a wish no filter serves, `unsupported`) are looked for in the listings' own text as the search's `q` and shown as one quiet, removable chip. When that would leave nothing they are dropped, one group at a time (the group whose going leaves the most listings first, the one written later on a tie), until something is found, and the page says which were left out, each with a way to put it back. The filters are never dropped: when they alone find nothing the words are kept as written and the page shows its no-results panel, with the filters as chips and what each removal would give. A city outside the market, text addressed to the system and a number no car has are said, never looked for. At most four groups are put to the count; the rest are named in one line. What was left out is derived again whenever the page is drawn (the code reading is milliseconds): a group the page does not look for and that would leave nothing is said; one the buyer took off themselves, which would find listings, is their choice and is not said.
- A number the buyer wrote is the buyer's own (digits in any script, number words, «میلیون»/«میلیارد»/«هزار», «و نیم», units). A model never supplies one; a value no car has is refused (a price under 20,000,000 tomans, a mileage above plausible, a year outside the calendar).
- Years: Gregorian minus 621; «زیر» is strict for a year; zero km is at most 100 km; a price or mileage with no relation word is at most.
- Tehran is the default market: no city filter, said once; a city the index lacks is said, never matched to another.
- A make or model the index does not collect is applied and said («هنوز جمع‌آوری نمی‌شود»), so the buyer is told rather than shown a wrong car; the model's Latin and Finglish names come from catalogue aliases.
- Intents map to documented filters and an order (`understand/intents.ts`, shown in the model's instructions); a filter the buyer stated is never changed by a bundle.
- Engine volume (CS-100, ADR-0039): read with its unit (`سی‌سی`, `cc`, `لیتر`, `لیتری`, Latin letters and digits) or after «حجم»/«موتور»; at least («بیشتر از», «بالای», «حداقل», «به بالا») is a minimum with the figure included, at most the figure, «کمتر از»/«زیر» strictly below, a figure alone or «حدود» five percent either side to the nearest ten, a range its ends, litres times 1,000, a volume outside 500 to 9,000 not applied and said. Origin: «خارجی»/«وارداتی» imported, «ایرانی»/«ساخت داخل» domestic and joint venture, «مونتاژ» joint venture; «تمیز» beside it is the clean bundle.
- Country (CS-103, ADR-0041): the adjectives and nouns «ژاپنی/ژاپن», «کره‌ای/کره جنوبی», «آلمانی», «چینی», «فرانسوی», «ایتالیایی», «آمریکایی/امریکایی», «انگلیسی/بریتانیایی», «سوئدی» (and the Latin and Finglish spellings japoni, koreie, almani, chini, faranse, amrikaii) are the brand's country, whoever assembled the car; they are read by code as soft phrases, when the query names cars (a car word, a catalogue name or another reading beside them) or nothing else is left, never in «رستوران ایتالیایی». «خارجی» stays origin imported and «ایرانی» origin domestic and joint venture. A country nobody lists is read and said (note `no_listings`), never an empty page without a word.
- Unsupported wishes (fuel economy, a sunroof) are looked for as words (above) and never invented as a filter.
- Instructions inside the sentence: sentences addressed to a system are removed before any model sees them and told to the buyer; hidden characters are told too; the sentence is cut at 200 characters and told.
- Confidence: `weak` model readings are suggestions only.

## The switch (ADR-0029, ADR-0043)

`SEARCH_UNDERSTANDING_AI` off (default): code only; nothing waits, no model is asked, and the page says nothing about a model. On: the model reads what code cannot settle, within the cache, 40 paid questions per address and hour, US$1 per Tehran day, 4 at once, 7 s. The page never waits for it. It is drawn from the code's reading, and from a model's answer too when the layer's cache already holds one for the sentence (`cachedModelStep`: a request that would be paid is refused, so drawing a page never spends). When none is cached and the address is exactly what the code read and settled, a small client leaf asks `POST /api/search/understand` in the background (one question per sentence per tab), under one quiet line («در حال خواندن بقیه‌ی جمله…», its height reserved); the answer carries `href`, the address its reading leads to, and the page replaces its own address with it (no history entry) when it differs. A buyer who changes anything meanwhile moves the address away from what the code read, the leaf is no longer drawn and a late answer is dropped: the buyer's choices stand. Whatever is held back is answered by code with `degraded.reason` (`switched_off`, `unavailable`, `visitor_limit`, `daily_cap`, `busy`, `timeout`, `invalid_answer`), which the route reports and the page does not show.

## Acceptance (measured in the evaluation report)

On 163 labelled queries (83 development, 80 test): code only 94.5 % fully right; with the model 96.9 % (test split run once: 93.8 %); 87 % need no model; 0 of 10 injection witnesses; cost about US$0.20 per 1,000 queries with the model; p50 of a model-asked query 2.8 to 3.5 s, p95 4.2 to 5.2 s.

On 270 labelled queries (the 163 above, 71 on volume and origin and 36 on country, the last labelled on 2026-10-04): code only 95.2 % fully right and all 36 country queries (12 of 12 held out); with the model 96.7 %; the model alone reads 31 of the 36 at US$1.80 per 1,000 queries (`docs/evidence/query-understanding/2026-10-04-country/report.md`). On 215 labelled queries (the 163 above and 52 on volume and origin, labelled on 2026-10-03 before the code read them): code only 95.8 % fully right and 100 % of the 52; with the model 97.7 % (the model was asked about none of the 52); the model alone reads 61.5 % of the 52 at US$1.94 per 1,000 queries; spend US$0.1335 (`docs/evidence/query-understanding/2026-10-03-engine-volume/report.md`).

## Out of scope

Typing-time suggestions and recent searches (CS-114), follow-up questions, voice. A search file keeps the search (filters and words), never the sentence.
