# ADR-0041: The country of a brand is a catalogue fact the superadmin keeps per make and per model, separate from where the car was built; «ژاپنی» is the brand's country whoever assembled the car

- Status: accepted by delegation (2026-10-04; the owner asked that lane decisions be taken without him, "decide yourself based on best you recommend")
- Date: 2026-10-04
- Deciders: Pedrum (delegated to the CS-99 lane)
- Related: CS-103, CS-99, CS-100; ADR-0023, ADR-0027, ADR-0028, ADR-0029, ADR-0039; `docs/evidence/query-understanding/2026-10-04-country/`

## Context

On 2026-10-04 the owner asked for «ماشین ژاپنی», «کره‌ای», «آلمانی», «چینی», «فرانسوی», «آمریکایی», besides «خارجی» (imported) and «ایرانی». ADR-0039 gave every model an origin: domestic (an Iranian design), joint venture (a foreign design built in Iran) or imported. That says where the car was built or sold from; it does not say which country the brand comes from, and the two answer different questions. A buyer who asks for a «ماشین فرانسوی» may be shown a Peugeot 206 built by Iran Khodro (it is French by design and its origin is joint venture); a buyer who asks for a «ماشین خارجی» wants cars that were brought in, which a Peugeot 206 is not.

## Decision

1. **Country is a separate attribute from origin**, held per make and, where a model differs, per model: `country_spec` has one row for a make (`model_id` NULL) or for one of its models (`UNIQUE NULLS NOT DISTINCT (make_id, model_id)`, the model's key joined to its make's by a composite foreign key), with a `country` from a closed list of lower-case ISO 3166-1 codes (ir, jp, kr, cn, de, fr, it, us, gb, se, cz, es, ro, ru, my, in, tw; a named check) and a `source` (`seed`, `superadmin`). The Farsi names and the words buyers write for each are code (`COUNTRIES` in `@carshenas/search/specs`), so a new country is a migration and a line there.
2. **The country is the brand's, whoever assembled the car.** «ژاپنی» matches Toyota, Honda, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Nissan, Suzuki and the other Japanese makes; a Peugeot 206 or 405 built in Iran is «فرانسوی»; MG is «چینی» (made and owned in China today, whatever its British history), Volvo «سوئدی», a Kia assembled here «کره‌ای». «خارجی» and «وارداتی» keep meaning origin imported, and «ایرانی» keeps meaning origin domestic or joint venture, never a country (Iran's own cars are found by it). A phrase may carry both: «ماشین خارجی ژاپنی» is imported and Japanese.
3. **A listing is given its model's country, else its make's**, in one view, `listing_spec`, which also holds the volume, origin and their sources (ADR-0039), so search (`listing_filter_row`), the listing page and the superadmin's coverage read the same inheritance. A make or model with no row is unknown; nothing is guessed, and a listing with an unknown country is excluded by a country filter and counted in the page's «N listings left out» line.
4. **The superadmin changes rows only through `set_country_spec()`** (SECURITY DEFINER, ADR-0023), which states the target (NULL removes the row) and appends `country_spec_change` (append-only, kept after the row is gone) with the earlier and later country, who and when. The web role reads the country and where it belongs, never who set it. A change marks the listings it covers (a make's, or one model's) for the next search refresh once each, in a statement-level trigger (ADR-0028). The screen is the specs section of `/admin/tracked-models`: the makes with no country (those with listings first), and for each model the country of its make and, where it differs, its own.
5. **Seed.** 121 makes: the Iranian makes (ir), the Japanese, Korean, German, French, Italian, American, British, Swedish, Czech, Spanish, Romanian, Russian, Malaysian, Taiwanese and the Chinese brands I am sure of. Brands whose country I am not sure of (Iranian-assembled Chinese designs under local names such as KMC, Fownix, Farda, Fidelity, Capra) have no row and are listed in the screen for the superadmin to fill. The make-level choice of a brand is a guess for the superadmin to review where a make sells cars of several countries.
6. **Search** has a `country` filter (a choice from the database, so only countries with listings are offered, with their counts, like the makes; URL `country=jp`; chip «کشور ژاپن»), `search_document.country` with the same inheritance and refresh path, and the facet `country` in `search_facet_count`.
7. **Plain-Farsi reading.** The adjectives and nouns (ژاپنی ژاپن، کره‌ای کره جنوبی، آلمانی، چینی، فرانسوی، ایتالیایی، آمریکایی امریکایی، انگلیسی بریتانیایی، سوئدی …) and their Latin and Finglish spellings (japoni, koreie, almani, chini, faranse, amrikaii) are read by code as soft phrases: read when the query is about cars (a car word, a catalogue name or another reading beside them) or when nothing else is left, and left alone in «رستوران ایتالیایی». A country nobody lists is read and said («فعلاً آگهی‌ای از «کره جنوبی» در کارشناس نیست»), never silently empty. The model behind the default-off switch has the same filter and the closed list of codes in its instructions and is told that a country word in a search that is not about cars gets no reading.

## Alternatives considered

- **One attribute for origin and country** (imported-from-Japan): the questions differ, and a buyer who says «خارجی» does not mean the French design built here.
- **A column on `make` and `model`**: no author, no history, and a model that corrects its make has nowhere to say so. Separate rows with a change record follow `model_spec` and `model_photo_link`.
- **A `country` table with the Farsi names**: the words buyers write are code anyway, and a new country needs a migration for either design; one closed list in code and a check in the database is one place fewer.
- **The country as the place of assembly** («ژاپنی» only for cars built in Japan): it would answer a Peugeot 206 «ایرانی», which is the origin's job, and it would split one brand's listings by factory, which nobody can tell from an ad.
- **Reading a country word as a hard phrase**: «رستوران ایتالیایی» (a label of the evaluation) became a filter on Italian cars; soft phrases with a car context keep the clarity of «ماشین ژاپنی» and refuse the restaurant.

## Consequences

- Positive: on the lane's data (a copy of main's older database) «ماشین ژاپنی» returns 117 searchable Toyota listings and «ماشین خارجی تمیز» 113, with chips and no text-search fallback; «ماشین فرانسوی کم‌کارکرد» 1,451; Korean, German, Chinese, American, Italian, British and Swedish cars have no listing there and the page says so. 36 of 36 labelled country queries are read as labelled by code, 12 of 12 held-out (a small set: the honest interval is 76 to 100 %).
- Negative / risks: the make-level seed is a first guess for makes that sell cars of several countries; a brand sold under a local name has no country until the superadmin gives one; a country change reaches search within a minute.
- After the merge, in main, in this order (all of ADR-0039's steps, which this ADR adds to): `pnpm db:migrate` (creates and seeds `country_spec` and `listing_spec`, adds the search columns and the facet); restart the worker; `pnpm derive:listings` (parser version 7: the volume a title states); `pnpm search:rebuild` (fills the search columns and the country facet). Nothing sends a request to any site.
- Follow-ups: a model-level country for the makes that sell cars of two countries; «اروپایی» and «آسیایی» as groups of countries; excluding a country («غیر ژاپنی»).
